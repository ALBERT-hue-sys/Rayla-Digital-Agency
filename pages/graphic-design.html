Overview

What graphic design means here.

A brand falls apart in the details. The logo is right, but the invoice template is not, the banner at the conference uses a different blue, and the Instagram post looks like a different company entirely.

Graphic design here means building a visual system and then applying it consistently: identity, colour, type and layout rules, plus the actual artwork you need day to day. Everything is delivered in editable and export-ready formats, print specifications included.

Whether you are starting an identity from nothing or bringing an inconsistent one back into line, the goal is the same — every surface your business appears on should look like it came from the same place.

Why It Matters

Consistency is what makes a business look established.

Customers never audit your brand guidelines. They simply notice, somewhere below conscious thought, that the invoice, the banner and the Instagram post do not look like the same company — and they price that in.

Inconsistency reads as improvisation. It is the cheapest possible way to look smaller and less reliable than you actually are.

A visual system fixes it once. Rather than redesigning every asset from nothing, the rules get set and everything after that is application.

Looking the size you are

A consistent system is what separates an established business from one that is visibly improvising.

No redesigning from scratch

Rules for colour, type and layout mean the next asset is application rather than invention.

Files your team can use

Editable and export-ready formats, so routine work does not bottleneck on us.

Print that comes back right

Bleed, colour mode and resolution are handled before it goes to the printer, not after.

How We Work

How a design job runs.

01

Brief

What has to be designed, where it will appear, and what it has to sit alongside.

02

Direction

Two or three visual routes to react to, so the direction is agreed before detail work begins.

03

Design

The chosen route built out properly, across the actual assets you need day to day.

04

Refine

Your notes applied in rounds agreed up front, so revisions do not become open-ended.

05

Handover

Editable source files, export-ready artwork, and the usage rules that keep it consistent.

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Do we get the source files?

Yes. You receive editable working files alongside the exports. The work is yours.

Can you work with our existing logo?

Yes. Plenty of projects are about building a proper system around a logo that already exists rather than replacing it.

How many revisions are included?

A defined number, agreed in the brief. In practice, a clear brief and an early review round matter far more than an unlimited revision count.

Do you handle the printing?

We prepare press-ready files and can coordinate with your printer on specifications. Printing itself is quoted separately.

What if we need something urgently?

Tell us the deadline up front. We will say honestly whether it is achievable rather than accept it and miss it.

A consultation is free and there is no obligation. Tell us what you are trying to achieve and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.
