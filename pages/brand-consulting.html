Overview

What brand consulting means here.

Brand consulting is the conversation that should happen before any money goes into advertising. What does this business actually stand for, who is it for, and why would someone choose it over the alternative?

Businesses that cannot answer those questions clearly end up paying to broadcast a vague message, then concluding that marketing does not work. It usually did work — it just amplified something that was not sharp enough to land.

This is advisory work, run as structured sessions with the people who make the decisions. The output is clarity you can act on: a position, a message, and a view on what to do next.

Why It Matters

Advertising amplifies whatever you give it.

If the message is vague, paid media makes the vagueness reach more people. That is usually what a business means when it says marketing did not work.

The questions are simple and uncomfortable: what does this business stand for, who is it for, and why would someone choose it over the alternative? Most teams have three different answers internally.

Settling that before you spend is the highest-leverage work available. Everything downstream — design, content, advertising — gets sharper and cheaper once the answer is fixed.

Spend against something specific

Advertising works far harder once there is a clear message underneath it to amplify.

One answer, not three

The team stops describing the business differently depending on who is asked.

A reason to be chosen

A position states plainly why someone should pick you over the obvious alternative.

Faster decisions

With the position settled, most day-to-day marketing questions answer themselves.

What’s Included

How We Work

How a consulting engagement runs.

01

Audit

We read everything you currently say about yourself, and note where it contradicts itself.

02

Interviews

Structured sessions with the people who make the decisions, and where useful, with customers.

03

Positioning

The choice of who you are for and what you stand against — argued, not asserted.

Articulation

05

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Who should be in these sessions?

Whoever actually decides. Consulting works badly when the person who can change direction is not in the room.

Is this useful for a small business?

Especially for a small business. Smaller budgets punish unclear positioning hardest, because there is no room to waste spend.

What do we walk away with?

A written summary of the positioning, messaging and recommended next steps — not just a conversation.

Do you then execute the recommendations?

We can, through the other services, but there is no obligation. The advice stands on its own.

How long is an engagement?

Anything from a single focused session to a series over several weeks, depending on how much is unresolved.
