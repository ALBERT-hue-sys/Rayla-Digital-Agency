About Us

About Rayla

Rayla Digital Agency is a creative marketing agency based in Kampala, Uganda. We partner with businesses, organisations and growing brands to create content and digital experiences that strengthen their presence and help them connect with their audiences.

Rather than chasing trends, we focus on understanding people. Every campaign, shoot and strategy begins with one question:

"What will make this audience care?"

That's the thinking behind every project we deliver.
