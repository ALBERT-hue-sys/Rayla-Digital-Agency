Questions

Answers before you ask.

The things brands want to know before the first call. If yours isn't here, ask us directly.

Talk to us
What do you handle in-house?

Content production, social media management, content strategy, graphic design, brand consulting and digital marketing all sit with our own team. We bring in specialists for niche production work — and we tell you when we do.

How long before we see results?

Paid campaigns produce usable data within days. Organic reach and search compound over months. We set that expectation at the start rather than after the first invoice.

Do you work with brands outside Uganda?

Yes. We are based in Kampala and run remote engagements across East Africa and further afield. Production shoots are the only part that needs us in the room.

Can we hire you for one project instead of a retainer?

Yes. Launches, single campaigns and production shoots all work as standalone projects. A retainer makes sense once you are publishing continuously and want the strategy to carry between campaigns.

Who owns the content you produce?

You do. Final assets, raw files and any accounts we set up are handed over and stay yours, whether or not we keep working together.

What does a project cost?

Scope sets the price — volume of content, number of channels, and how much strategy the work needs up front. We quote after one conversation, not before it.
